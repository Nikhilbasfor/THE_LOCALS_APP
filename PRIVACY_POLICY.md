# Privacy Policy for THE LOCALS

**Last updated:** September 19, 2026

Welcome to **THE LOCALS** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use our mobile application (**THE LOCALS-Experiences**).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

When you register and use the application as a Traveller or Local Guide, we may collect the following information:

* **Account & Identity Information:** Name, email address, phone number, and account role (Traveller or Guide).
* **Guide Verification Details:** Bio, profile photos, operating states/destinations, years of experience, and identification documents provided to verify credentials.
* **Bookings & Transactions:** Details of tours booked, itineraries, dates, participant numbers, and transaction IDs (payment card/banking details are handled securely by PCI-compliant payment gateways like Razorpay and are never stored on our servers).
* **Location Data:** With your explicit permission, we access your approximate or precise location to suggest nearby local guides and curated travel experiences.
* **Communications:** Messages exchanged between travellers and guides within the app to coordinate tours.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
* To create and manage your user account.
* To facilitate seamless booking between travellers and local guides.
* To verify the authenticity and safety of registered guides.
* To send order confirmations, booking updates, and essential service alerts.
* To provide responsive customer support and resolve disputes.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers essential for app operations:
* **Google Firebase / Google Cloud:** For secure user authentication, database management, and cloud storage.
* **Payment Processors (Razorpay):** For end-to-end encrypted and PCI-DSS compliant payment processing.
* **Apple:** For app distribution and in-app crash reporting.

---

## 4. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

In compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines:
* You have full control over your personal data.
* You can request account and data deletion at any time directly within the app by going to **Profile Settings ➔ Delete Account**.
* Alternatively, you can email our support team at [prakashsharma0921@gmail.com](mailto:prakashsharma0921@gmail.com) with the subject "Data Deletion Request". All associated profile data, bookings history, and authentication credentials will be permanently erased from our active databases within 30 days.

---

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption, SSL protocols, and Firebase security rules to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

THE LOCALS is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
